Title: Headline Match

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable pairwise headline review. Independent addresses submit one headline and its declared promise against a frozen article and supported-facts boundary. GenLayer validators compare named pairs using LEFT, RIGHT, or TIE plus a closed reason code. Pair uniqueness and win totals are deterministic, while the human editor alone selects a candidate with a recorded win. The contract neither browses nor publishes content.
